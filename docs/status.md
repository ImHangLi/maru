# Status

Updated: 2026-10-14.

Maru was developed in a private repository before it became public. Issue numbers, pull request numbers, and commit IDs in `docs/decisions.md` and `docs/research/` refer to that private history. This repository starts from one clean commit.

## Current state

- The app and the package build. CI builds and tests the package, builds the app for the iOS Simulator, and builds the Watch app for the watchOS Simulator.
- Milestone A (the core loop on one iPhone) is complete in code: add and edit an item, Today, records, undo, history, item detail, settings, local reminders, optional follow-up reminders, and as-needed items.
- The app has six languages (D059): English, zh-Hans, ja, es, de, and ko. The translation is not reviewed by a native speaker yet (`docs/localization-review.md`). Nobody saw it on a display.
- The design rules are in `docs/design.md`. Native SwiftUI components only (D026).
- Maru does not integrate with Apple Health in V1 (D022).
- An "every other day" schedule is in code (D036). Its reminders are dated requests for the next 7 due dates, and they need a check on a real iPhone.
- Two more schedules are in code (D065, T52): "Every Few Days" (every 3 to 30 days) and "Days On and Off" (for example 21 days on and 7 days off), with a first day that the user chooses. They use the rule and the dated requests of D036, and migration `v5` of the database. Nobody saw them on a display. They need a check on a real iPhone: the menus that start on "Choose", the date picker "Another Date" with a past date, the plan line, no row on Today and no notification in the pause, the "Next Dose" row after a pause, and the six languages. The reminders of a plan with a pause can stop after one week with no open of Maru, and the item detail says so. The site FAQ, `site/llms.txt`, and the store description name them. If they ship in 1.1, remove them from the site and the store text for 1.0. The feature does not block the first release. If the check on the iPhone is not done, it ships in 1.1.
- Quick add is in code (D038): "Scan Label" on the add screen reads a name from a label with the camera, on the iPhone, and the name field proposes names from two built-in lists (941 supplement names, 789 medicine names). After the first test on the iPhone, the scan card shows the words of a tapped text as buttons, and the user chooses the words and edits the name on the card (D042). Nobody saw the new card yet, and it needs a check on a real iPhone with real bottles.
- An export of the records as a CSV file, and a backup and a restore, are in code (D040). `docs/export-format.md` has the formats. The files are not encrypted. Nobody ran them on a real iPhone yet: the share sheet, the file picker, and a restore after a new install need that check.
- Each language other than English has its own name lists (D070): about 165 to 185 supplements and 200 to 250 medicines in zh-Hans, ja, ko, es, and de, before the English lists. A local supplement name gets the smart completion of its English twin. The debug sample data follows the app language. A first review corrected some names. Every name still needs a check by a native pharmacist (`docs/localization-review.md`), and nobody saw the suggestions on a display.
- Smart completion for supplements is in code (D043): a supplement name from the built-in list sets the usual form until the user chooses one, and the amount, the reminder, and the note show candidates that fill nothing until a tap. The list has 103 supplements. Nobody saw the screen yet.
- History has a graph of squares (D041): one square per day, three levels of the tint by the share taken, and 13, 26, or 52 weeks. A touch or a drag selects a day, and the end of the touch opens the log of that day (D054). Under the graph, "Last 7 Days" has one row for each item with a strip of 7 capsules, and a tap opens the log of the item (D054). The long timeline left the main page. Nobody saw it on a display yet. The drag, the haptic, the dark-mode levels, the strip at the largest text sizes, the alerts from a log, and VoiceOver need a check on a real iPhone.
- Today never folds (D057): every part of the day always shows its rows, and its header is the plain title. The item detail shows the same 7-day strip as History in place of the list of records. Nobody saw either on a display yet.
- A consistency pass is in code (D058): Title Case alert titles, one empty-state pattern, one word for each action, one-sentence footers, states in the text color with a symbol, one sheet size rule, photos in History rows, one day format, named press and fill motion, and a translator comment on every String Catalog key. Nobody built or saw it on a display yet. CI builds the app. It needs a check on a real iPhone: the press scale and the fill spring (also with Reduce Motion), the as-needed dose row at the largest text sizes, and the sheet heights.
- A copy review changed text in the six languages (D068): the text states the record and not the intake, the widget "Record Dose" has its new name, and a widget capsule tells an open dose from a skipped dose to VoiceOver. Nobody saw the new text on a display. The Japanese, Korean, Spanish, and German text is a draft until a native speaker reads it.
- Today rows show the time on the second line (D044): the set time of an open row, the intake time of a taken row, and "Skipped". Nobody saw the rows on a display yet.
- The user sets the follow-ups (D039): 1, 2, 3, or 5 times, or "Until Recorded" with at most 12 for each dose, also across a change of the setting, and 5 minutes to 1 hour between them. Follow-ups continue after midnight until 01:00, and their "Taken" action records the dose of the day before. Nobody saw them on a device yet. They need a check on a real iPhone: the times, the stop after a record, the group in Notification Center, a Focus, and the action after midnight.
- Reminders skip a recorded dose and give one notification for each fire time (D047): a dose recorded before its time does not remind, two items at one time give one notification that names the open items, and a daily item and an every-other-day item share one request on a due day. Nobody saw it on a device yet. It needs a check on a real iPhone: record before the time and no notification, undo before the time and the notification comes, one of two items recorded and the text names the other one with the "Taken" action.
- Product analytics are in code (D052), with the rule of D051 and D078: on by default outside the EU, the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, with no notice, and opt-in inside them, with a one-time question. D077 adds events and fields for product decisions (in the pull request `leon/analytics-v2`, for a build 4). The build has no key, so the app has no switch and sends nothing until a release build gets `MARU_ANALYTICS_KEY`. Each event also has the region setting of the iPhone as a two-letter code, and no location (D064). Nobody saw the opt-in question or the switch, and nobody sent an event to a real PostHog project. The PostHog project exists, with "Discard client IP data" on. The privacy label is published in App Store Connect (D075). Before the first release: check the packets (T42) with a build that has the key. The server data of a user cannot be deleted from the app yet (D052).
- The optional item photo is in code (D056): the photo choice beside the form and the color on the add and edit screen (D073), a photo from the library or the camera, a background removal on the device that never blocks Save, and the photo in place of the form icon on Today, My Items, the item detail, and the archived list. The backup does not hold the photo files. Nobody saw it on a display. It needs a check on a real iPhone: the camera, the background removal with one bottle, a bottle in a hand, and two bottles (T29), a Cancel during the removal (T30), a missing file, and a locked iPhone.
- The app also runs on iPad (D072): both device families, the four orientations on the iPad, Split View and Slide Over, and an iPad form of the 13 strings that name the iPhone. The iPad uses the layout of the iPhone at a larger width: the same tabs, pages, and pushes, and the content fills the window. A first two-column layout was removed at the maintainer's request. Each iPad has its own local database, and Maru has no sync. The backup file moves the data between two devices, without the photos. Nobody saw the current layout on an iPad, in a simulator or on a device. It needs a check on a real iPad before the first upload, because an update cannot remove the iPad later. `PRIVACY.md`, the site, and `docs/faq.md` name the iPad.
- Three flow gaps against Apple Health Medications are closed in code (D033): a change of a record time in three taps, an archive in three taps with one confirmation, and a delete of an item with its records. Nobody saw them on a display yet.
- Optional supporter tips are in code (D053): three consumable tips in a "Support Maru" section in Settings, with StoreKit 2. Nothing is locked, and a tip never touches health data. Nobody ran it. It needs a check with the StoreKit configuration (`Config/Maru.storekit`): a buy, a cancel, a pending tip, a failure, and a refund. App Store Connect has the three products with names and prices (D075). They have their review screenshot. They are not in the submission of 1.0.0 (`docs/release-plan.md`, section 7). The maintainer does not register for GST for now, so the tips may not be for sale in the Australian storefront (D064).
- The App Store listing is in `metadata/` and in App Store Connect (D075): name, subtitle, keywords, promotional text, and description in six languages, es-MX, and en-GB, in the layout of `asc metadata`. `docs/research/2026-10-13-aso-and-store-page.md` has the study, six screenshot frames for each language, and the drafts of the privacy label, the age rating, and the review notes. `docs/release-plan.md`, section 7, has every setting of the record. The secondary category is Medical. Mainland China is not in the first release. No native speaker read the text. The screenshots are uploaded for the eight store languages, for the iPhone and the 13-inch iPad. The support and privacy pages are live.

- A first Apple Watch app is in code (D071): one screen with the doses of today by part of the day, a tap for "Taken", and a swipe for "Skip" and "Undo". The Watch has no database. It asks the iPhone for each record and shows "recorded" only after the iPhone confirms the commit. Nobody built it on a Mac or ran it on a Watch. CI builds it with the app. The database is now readable on a locked iPhone after the first unlock (D066), so a record from the Watch, and the "Taken" action of a reminder on the Watch, can work with the iPhone locked. Nobody saw this on a device. Version 1.0 ships without it (D074): the app does not embed the Watch app, and `PRIVACY.md` does not describe it. It waits for 1.1 and a test on a real Watch.

- The app tint is not on row text any more, Settings has no test notification, and the medium "Today" widget has a progress bar in place of the count (D076). A simulator capture confirmed Settings, the add and edit screen, and the widget layout in light and dark. Nobody saw the widget on a Home Screen, in the tinted or clear look, or the "Item Options…" row of the item detail.

- Build 3 of version 1.0.0 is the submission build. It is on TestFlight for the internal group and on the App Store version, without the Watch app (D074, D075). The screenshots are uploaded for the eight store languages. The tips are ready, but they are not in the submission of 1.0.0: the first tip goes to review with a later version, after the Paid Apps agreement is active. The app is not submitted for review, and the maintainer starts the submission. `docs/release-plan.md`, section 7, has the details.
