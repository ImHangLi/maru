# Questions and answers

These are the questions that people ask most about reminder apps. The list comes from a study of user feedback (`docs/research/2026-10-11-user-feedback-cn.md`). Use these answers for the App Store text and the README.

## Why not Apple Health or an alarm?

Maru is one small place for one job. You open it, and the first screen shows what to take now. One tap records it. Apple Health keeps the same job several levels inside a large app. An alarm rings, but it does not know what you took.

## What if I forget to record?

Nothing happens. Maru has no streaks and no penalties. A record is there to help you, not to judge you. You can also record later, or change the time of a record.

## Did I take it or not?

Today keeps each recorded item in view with its mark. History shows every day, with what you took, what you skipped, and what has no record.

## Can Maru keep reminding me?

Yes. In Settings, "Remind Again" sends more reminders for a dose that you did not record. You choose how many, and how long between them, from 5 minutes to 1 hour. "Until Recorded" reminds you up to 12 times for each dose, on the same day, and stops when you record. iPhone does not let an app remind with no end, so Maru states the number. Maru is not an alarm: a Focus or silent mode can hold a reminder.

## Is there a limit on the number of items?

No. You can add as many items as you want. Maru has no account and no paywall on the core.

## Where is my data?

On your iPhone. Maru has no server and makes no network requests by itself. The feedback form in Settings is a web page that opens only when you tap it. Read `PRIVACY.md` for the details.

## Does Maru count my stock or tell me to buy more?

No. Maru reminds and records. It does not track stock. This keeps the app small and quick.

## Can Maru tell me what to take or how much?

No. Maru does not give medical advice. You enter what you take, and Maru reminds you. The name suggestions on the add screen are a typing aid. They are not medical advice.

## Does the label scan send a photo anywhere?

No. Your iPhone reads the text on the label. Maru keeps only the name that you tap, and it saves no photo.
