# Questions and answers

These are the questions that people ask most about reminder apps. The list comes from a study of user feedback (`docs/research/2026-10-11-user-feedback-cn.md`). Use these answers for the App Store text and the README.

## Why not Apple Health or an alarm?

Maru is one small place for one job. You open it, and the first screen shows what to take now. One tap records it. Apple Health keeps the same job several levels inside a large app. An alarm rings, but it does not know what you took.

## What if I forget to record?

You can record later, or change the time of a record.

## Did I take it or not?

Today keeps each recorded item in view with its mark. History shows every day, with what you took, what you skipped, and what has no record.

## Can Maru keep reminding me?

Yes. On the screen of an item, "Remind Again" sends more reminders for a dose that you did not record. Each item has its own setting. You choose how many, and how long between them, from 5 minutes to 1 hour. "Until Recorded" reminds you up to 12 times for each dose, until 1:00 AM at the latest, and stops when you record. A change of the setting does not add more than 12 for a dose. iOS does not let an app remind with no end, so Maru states the number. A reminder is a notification, so a Focus or silent mode can hold it. With iOS 26, you can also turn on "Alarm" for an item. Its dose time then rings like the alarm of the Clock app, also in silent mode and in a Focus.

## Is there a limit on the number of items?

No. You can add as many items as you want. Maru is free and needs no account.

## Where is my data?

On your iPhone or iPad. Each device has its own data. Maru has no server and no account. Optional usage statistics never include item names, doses, or times, and a switch in Settings turns them off. The feedback form in Settings is a web page that opens only when you tap it. Read `PRIVACY.md` for the details.

## Can I take my records with me?

Yes. "Export Records" in Settings makes a CSV file with every record. Numbers and Excel open it. You choose where the file goes. Maru sends nothing by itself.

## How do I move to a new iPhone or iPad?

On the old device, tap "Back Up" in Settings and save the file, for example to iCloud Drive. On the new device, tap "Restore from Backup…" and pick the file. The restore replaces the data of Maru on the new device. The file is not encrypted, so keep it private. A transfer or a backup of the whole device by iOS can also bring the data of Maru. Maru has no sync between two devices.

## Does Maru count my stock or tell me to buy more?

No. Maru reminds and records. It does not track stock. This keeps the app small and quick.

## Can Maru tell me what to take or how much?

No. Maru does not give medical advice. You enter what you take, and Maru reminds you. The name suggestions on the add screen are a typing aid. For common supplements, the add screen also shows amounts, times, and notes from common labels. They fill nothing until you tap, and they are not medical advice.

## Does the label scan send a photo anywhere?

No. Your device reads the text on the label. Maru keeps only the name that you tap, and it saves no photo.
