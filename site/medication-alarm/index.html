A medication alarm that rings in Silent mode and Focus

A normal reminder is a notification, so Silent mode or a Focus can hold it. On iOS 26 or later, Maru can ring like the Clock app at the time of a dose.

How it works

  • Open an item and turn on Alarm. You can also turn it on for all items in Settings.
  • The first time, iOS asks if Maru can use alarms. Allow it.
  • At the time of the dose, the alarm rings, also in Silent mode and in a Focus.
  • For one item, the alarm has a Taken button that records the dose. For several items, it has Open Maru.
  • If you record the dose before its time, the alarm does not ring that day.

It needs iOS 26 or later

Maru uses the alarm system of iOS 26. On iOS 17 to iOS 25, the Alarm switch is not there. Maru then sends normal reminders, and Remind Again can send up to 12 more reminders for a dose that you did not record.

Why not the Clock app?

A Clock alarm rings, but it does not know what you took. It rings again on a day when you already took your medicine. A Maru alarm belongs to the dose, and it records the dose when you tap Taken.

The alarm is free

Alarms and Remind Again are free in Maru, with no limit on items. Maru needs no account and has no ads.
