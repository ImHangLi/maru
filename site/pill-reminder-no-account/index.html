A pill reminder with no account and no ads

Open Maru and add what you take. There is no sign-up, no email, and no ad.

No account

Maru has no login and no profile on a server. A purchase of Maru Pro goes through your Apple Account and does not make a Maru account. A person or a pet in Family is a label on your iPhone or iPad, not an account.

No ads

Maru shows no ads, also without Maru Pro.

Your records stay on your device

Your items and records stay on your iPhone or iPad. Maru has no server for them. With Maru Pro, your iPhone sends the list of today to your own paired Apple Watch.

Simple usage statistics never include what you take, and one switch in Settings turns them off. That is why the App Store privacy label of Maru lists data linked to you, for analytics: purchases, identifiers, usage data, diagnostics, and other data. The privacy page has every detail.

A new iPhone or iPad

With no account, nothing moves by itself. In Maru's Settings on the old device, tap Back Up and save the file. On the new device, tap Restore from Backup and choose that file. The file is not encrypted, so keep it private.

What is free

Reminders, alarms, Remind Again, records, History, undo, widgets, Export Records, and backups are free, with no limit on items. Maru Pro is optional.
