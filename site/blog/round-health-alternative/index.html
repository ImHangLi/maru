A Round Health alternative

On November 6, 2026, Round Health was not in the US App Store. If you look for a new pill reminder for iPhone, this page tells what Maru does and how to start.

By the Maru team · Published November 6, 2026 · 2 min read

The Maru team makes Maru. Facts about other apps were checked on November 6, 2026. The sources are at the end of this page.

What we checked

Apple's search service gave no result for the Round Health app ID in the US store, and the old store address showed no page. We do not know why the app left the store, and we do not compare its features, because we could not read its store page.

What Maru does

  • It reminds you at the times that you choose. Remind Again sends up to 12 more reminders if you do not record the dose.
  • On iOS 26 or later, an item can have an alarm that rings in Silent mode and Focus, like the Clock app.
  • You record from the reminder: Taken, Skip, or Remind in 10 min. Maru stays closed.
  • Today shows what you took and what is still to take. History shows months of records, with no streaks.
  • No account and no ads. Your items and records stay on your iPhone or iPad.
  • With Maru Pro: an Apple Watch app, your next dose on the Lock Screen, PDF reports, and Family for people and pets.

Start with Maru

  • Add an item: type its name, and choose the times and the days.
  • Allow notifications, so Maru can remind you.
  • When the reminder comes, tap Taken.

Maru cannot read a file from another app, so you add your items one time. Maru does not suggest a dose or a time.
