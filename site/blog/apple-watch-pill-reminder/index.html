Record your pills from your Apple Watch

With Maru Pro, the Maru app on Apple Watch shows the doses of today. Tap a dose, and it is recorded.

By the Maru team · Published November 6, 2026 · 2 min read

What the Watch app does

  • It shows the doses of today by the part of the day: what you took and what is still to take.
  • A tap on an open dose records Taken.
  • A swipe gives Skip on an open dose, and Undo on a recorded dose.

How it works

Your iPhone keeps every record. The Watch app has no database. It sends each tap to your iPhone, and it shows the dose as recorded only after your iPhone saved it. The list of today goes from your iPhone to your own paired Watch, with no server in between.

So your iPhone must be in reach. After your iPhone restarts, unlock it once before you record from the Watch.

What you need

  • Maru on your iPhone, with Maru Pro.
  • An Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later, paired with that iPhone.

Maru or the Medications app on the Watch?

Apple Watch also has a Medications app that logs the medicines of Apple Health. Maru and Apple Health do not share data. Read the comparison of Maru and Apple Health.

Did I take it today?

Maru reminds you at the times you choose and records a dose with one tap. No account and no ads.
