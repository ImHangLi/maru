Maru vs MyTherapy

MyTherapy is a health diary: medicine, measurements, symptoms, and reports. Maru does one small job: it shows what to take now, and one tap records it.

By the Maru team · Published November 6, 2026 · 3 min read

The Maru team makes Maru. Facts about other apps were checked on November 6, 2026. The sources are at the end of this page.

MyTherapy can be the better choice

  • You want to track measurements such as blood pressure and weight, or keep a symptom diary.
  • You want refill and prescription reminders.
  • You want streaks to keep you on track.
  • You need a language that Maru does not have.

Maru can be the better choice

  • You want no ads, with no purchase.
  • You want your doses and nothing else on the first screen.
  • You want an alarm that rings in Silent mode and Focus. Maru has one on iOS 26 or later.
  • You do not want streaks. If you forget to record, nothing happens.
  • You want to record from your Apple Watch. Maru Pro has a Watch app.

Move from MyTherapy to Maru

Maru cannot read a file from another app, so you add your items in Maru one time. Each item needs a name and the times that you choose. Maru does not suggest a dose or a time.
