A reminder for your vitamins and supplements

Vitamin D in the morning, magnesium at night, a probiotic every other day. Maru reminds you, and one look at Today tells you what you took.

By the Maru team · Published November 6, 2026 · 2 min read

Add each one once

Type the name, then choose the times and the days. You can take or choose a photo of your own bottle, and it becomes the item icon. Maru can remove the background on your iPhone or iPad, and the photo stays on your device.

Maru does not suggest an amount or a time. You enter what is on your label or what your doctor told you.

Morning, noon, and evening

Today groups your doses by the part of the day. One reminder can cover several items at the same time, and Take All records them together.

Every other day, or with a break

Some supplements are not daily. Choose Every Other Day, Every Few Days (every 3 to 30 days), or Days On and Off, for example 5 days on and 2 off. An As Needed item has no reminders, and you record it when you take it.

No streaks

If you forget to record, nothing happens. You can record later or change the time. History shows months at a glance, with no streaks and no penalties.

For your family and your pets

With Maru Pro, add a person or a pet, such as a parent or your dog. Each one has a color, and Today and History can show one of them. Records stay on your iPhone or iPad, with no shared profiles and no sync.

What you get, and what Maru Pro adds

Reminders, alarms, records, History, widgets, Export Records, and backups are free, with no limit on items. No account and no ads. Maru Pro is optional.

Did I take it today?

Maru reminds you at the times you choose and records a dose with one tap. No account and no ads.
