A pill reminder app that shows what you took

Maru reminds you at the times that you choose. One tap records the dose. One look tells you if you took it today.

Reminders that keep going

Maru sends a reminder at each time that you set. Turn on Remind Again, and Maru sends up to 12 more reminders for a dose that you did not record, from 5 minutes to 1 hour apart. It stops when you record.

On iOS 26 or later, turn on Alarm for an item. The dose time then rings like the Clock app, also in Silent mode and in a Focus. Read more about the medication alarm.

Record from the reminder

A reminder for one item has Taken, Skip, and Remind in 10 min. A reminder for several items has Take All and Skip All. Maru stays closed.

Did I take it today?

Today shows each dose of the day: taken, skipped, or still to take. The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show your next dose. History shows months of records and the last seven days of each item. You can undo a record or change its time. Maru has no streaks and no penalties.

Schedules

Choose Every Day, Every Other Day, Every Few Days (every 3 to 30 days), or Days On and Off, for example 21 days on and 7 off. An As Needed item has no reminders, and you record it when you take it.

What is free

Maru is free to download. Reminders, alarms, Remind Again, records, History, undo, widgets, Export Records, and backups are free, with no limit on items. Maru Pro is optional. It adds Next Dose on Lock Screen, the Apple Watch app, PDF reports, 30-day counts, Family, app icons, reminder sounds, and app colors. The FAQ has the prices.

What Maru does not do

  • It does not count the pills that you have left.
  • It does not suggest a dose or a time, and it gives no medical advice.
  • It does not sync between devices. Each iPhone or iPad keeps its own data.
