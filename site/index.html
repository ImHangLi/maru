Maru: Know What You Took

A small iPhone app for daily supplements and simple medication schedules. It reminds you on time, shows what to take now, and keeps a record you can trust.

Coming soon to theApp Store

Maru is in early development and is not on the App Store yet.

Did I take it?

Today shows what to take now. One tap records a dose. History shows each day, so you do not have to guess.

Too many bottles

Put every supplement in one list. Each part of the day has its own section. Record a whole section with one tap.

Apps that nag or lock features

A missed dose is only a missed dose. No streaks, no scores, no guilt. The core has no paywall and no limit on items.

What Maru does not do

  • No account
  • No ads
  • No ad tracking
  • No cloud: your health data stays on the iPhone
  • No stock counting
  • No medical advice

Questions

Why not Apple Health or an alarm?

Maru is one small place for one job. You open it, and the first screen shows what to take now. One tap records it. Apple Health keeps the same job several levels inside a large app. An alarm rings, but it does not know what you took.

What if I forget to record?

Nothing happens. Maru has no streaks and no penalties. A record is there to help you, not to judge you. You can also record later, or change the time of a record.

Did I take it or not?

Today keeps each recorded item in view with its mark. History shows every day, with what you took, what you skipped, and what has no record.

Can Maru keep reminding me?

Yes. In Settings, "Remind Again" sends more reminders for a dose that you did not record. You choose how many, and how long between them, from 5 minutes to 1 hour. "Until Recorded" reminds you up to 12 times for each dose, until 1:00 AM at the latest, and stops when you record. A change of the setting does not add more than 12 for a dose. iOS does not let an app remind with no end, so Maru states the number. Maru is not an alarm: a Focus or silent mode can hold a reminder.
