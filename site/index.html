What Maru does

One small place for one job.

No dashboards. No scores. Open it, see what to take, and tap once.

Today

See what to take now.

The first screen is Today. Each part of the day has its own section, and each item shows its amount and its time.

  • Tap the circle to record a dose.
  • Record an as-needed item at the current time.
  • Change the time of a record, or undo it.

Reminders

On time. Out of the way.

Reminders are local notifications at the times you choose. Maru never picks a time for you. They do not go through a server.

  • Tap "Taken" in the reminder to record a dose with no open of Maru.
  • Turn on "Remind Again" for a dose that you did not record. It is off by default.
  • A medication can keep the clock of its home time zone when you travel.

Built on trust

Private, plain, and open.

Your health data stays on your iPhone.

Items, schedules, and records live in a local database that iOS protects while the iPhone is locked. Maru has no account and no server for your records. It contains no advertising or crash-reporting code.

Six languages

English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, German, and Korean. Reminders also use your language.

Made for everyone

Text at every size, VoiceOver, Dark Mode, and an app color that you choose.

Your choices only

Maru never picks a dose or a medication time for you. You enter what you take, and Maru reminds you.

Optional by design

Simple usage statistics and three tips are optional. Statistics never include item names, doses, or times, and a switch in Settings turns them off. A tip unlocks nothing.

What Maru does not do.

  • No account
  • No ads
  • No ad tracking
  • No cloud for your health data
  • No stock counting
  • No medical advice

Questions

Short answers.

Maru is one small place for one job. You open it, and the first screen shows what to take now. One tap records it. Apple Health keeps the same job several levels inside a large app. An alarm rings, but it does not know what you took.

Nothing happens. Maru has no streaks and no penalties. A record is there to help you, not to judge you. You can also record later, or change the time of a record.

Today keeps each recorded item in view with its mark. History shows every day, with what you took, what you skipped, and what has no record.

Yes. In Settings, "Remind Again" sends more reminders for a dose that you did not record. You choose how many, and how long between them, from 5 minutes to 1 hour. "Until Recorded" reminds you up to 12 times for each dose, until 1:00 AM at the latest, and stops when you record. A change of the setting does not add more than 12 for a dose. iPhone does not let an app remind with no end, so Maru states the number. Maru is not an alarm: a Focus or silent mode can hold a reminder.
