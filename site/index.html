How Maru helps

One small place for one job.

No dashboards. No scores. Open it, see what to take, and tap once.

Today

See what to take right now.

Open Maru and Today shows your day: morning, afternoon, evening. Tap the circle when you take something.

  • Each item shows how much and when.
  • Taking something only when needed? Record it at that moment.
  • Tapped by mistake? Undo it, or change the time.

Reminders

A nudge at the right time.

Maru reminds you at the times you choose. Tap Taken on the reminder, and Maru records it. You do not need to open the app.

  • You choose every time. Maru never picks one for you.
  • Forgot? Turn on Remind Again for more nudges. It is off by default.
  • Travelling? A medication can keep the clock of its home time zone.

History

Never wonder if you already took it.

See every day at a glance: what you took, what you skipped, and what has no record.

  • A strip of seven small capsules shows the last week of each item.
  • Fix a record any time. Undo it, or change the time.
  • Missed one? That is fine. No streaks, no scores, no guilt.

Widget

Check from your Home Screen.

Put a Maru widget on your Home Screen. One tap opens Maru.

  • By default the widget shows no item names. You choose to show more.
  • The widget can be out of date. Open Maru for the exact state.

Each item

Everything about one item.

Open an item to see its reminder, its schedule, and its last seven days.

  • Keep your records safe: export them as a file, or back up and restore your data.
  • Add a photo to an item if you like. It stays on your iPhone.

Good to know

Simple, private, and kind.

Your health data stays on your iPhone.

Your items and records live on your phone, not on a server. There is no account to make. There are no ads.

Read the privacy page

Free to use

The core of Maru is free, with no limit on items. Three optional tips let you say thanks. A tip unlocks nothing.

Calm, not pushy

A missed dose is only a missed dose. No streaks, no scores, and no guilt.

Your choices only

Maru never picks a dose or a medication time for you. You enter what you take, and Maru reminds you.

Made for everyone

Text at every size, VoiceOver, Dark Mode, and an app color that you choose.

Six languages

English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, German, and Korean. Reminders also use your language.

Questions

Short answers.

Maru is one small place for one job. You open it, and the first screen shows what to take now. One tap records it. Apple Health keeps the same job several levels inside a large app. An alarm rings, but it does not know what you took.

Nothing happens. Maru has no streaks and no penalties. A record is there to help you, not to judge you. You can also record later, or change the time of a record.

The core of Maru is free. It has no account, no ads, and no limit on the number of items. Settings has three optional tips. A tip unlocks nothing.

On your iPhone. Maru has no server and no account. Optional usage statistics never include item names, doses, or times, and a switch in Settings turns them off. The feedback form in Settings is a web page that opens only when you tap it. Read the privacy page for the details.

Yes. Maru is open source under the MIT license. You can read the code on GitHub.
