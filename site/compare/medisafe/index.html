Maru vs Medisafe

Both apps remind you to take your medicine and keep a record. Medisafe does more for prescriptions. Maru does less, needs no account, and keeps your records on your iPhone or iPad.

The Maru team makes Maru. Facts about other apps were checked on November 6, 2026. The sources are at the end of this page.

Medisafe can be the better choice

  • You want warnings about drug interactions. Medisafe shows them in the US.
  • You want refill reminders, or you want to track blood pressure, glucose, or weight in the same app.
  • You care for family members on other phones. Medisafe can sync the pillboxes of a family to one place.
  • You want to import your prescriptions from Health Records on iPhone, in the US.

Maru can be the better choice

  • You take vitamins, supplements, or medicine on a simple schedule, and you want to know at a glance if you took them today.
  • You do not want an account. Open Maru and add what you take.
  • You want reminders and records with no subscription. They are free in Maru, with no limit on items.
  • You want an alarm that rings in Silent mode and Focus. Maru has one on iOS 26 or later.
  • You want to record from the reminder: Taken, Skip, or Remind in 10 min, and Maru stays closed.
  • You use an iPad.

Move from Medisafe to Maru

Maru cannot read a file from another app, so you add your items in Maru one time. Each item needs a name and the times that you choose. Maru does not suggest a dose or a time.

Medisafe's help says that you can export a report of your history from "Support and data management". Keep that report if you want your old records.
