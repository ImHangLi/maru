Maru vs Apple Health Medications

Medications is part of the Health app on iPhone and iPad, and it reminds and logs. Apple Health does much more than Maru. Maru does one small job: it shows what to take now, and one tap records it.

The Maru team makes Maru. Facts about other apps were checked on November 6, 2026. The sources are at the end of this page.

Apple Health can be the better choice

  • You want your medicine in the same place as your other health data.
  • You want warnings about drug interactions, in the US.
  • You do not want to install another app.

Maru can be the better choice

  • You want the doses of now on the first screen, with one tap to record.
  • You want more than one extra reminder for a dose that you did not record.
  • You want an alarm that rings like the Clock app, on iOS 26 or later.
  • You keep the doses of a parent or a pet on your iPhone or iPad. Family is part of Maru Pro.
  • You want Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets with your next dose, and a CSV file of every record.

Can I use both?

Yes, but they do not share data. Maru does not read or write Apple Health. A dose that you record in one app does not show in the other.
