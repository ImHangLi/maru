What Maru does

  • A pill reminder app that shows what you took

    Reminders at your times, one tap to record, and a clear record of today.

  • A reminder for your vitamins and supplements

    Morning vitamins, evening magnesium, and every other day. One look at today.

  • A medication alarm that rings in Silent mode and Focus

    On iOS 26 or later, a dose can ring like the Clock app. Tap Taken to record it.

  • Record your pills from your Apple Watch

    See the doses of today on your wrist and record them with a tap.

  • A pill reminder with no account and no ads

    Open the app and add what you take. Your records stay on your device.
